# Illustration prompts for Gemini — set 1

A **creature of its own** for each of the 74 cards (US-017, then US-077 for cards 72 to 74), generated by Gemini (web application). The file is
produced from the catalogue (`packages/domain/src/set-1.ts`) and the
[art direction](direction-artistique.md); the prompts are in English, the language image generators follow
best.

## The brief

The first attempt ("server-room machine creature", same isometric framing as the first image) always gave
the same robot seen from the front, in an archway, with a halo: only the colour changed. From now on, the reference
image conveys **only the rendering** (line, soft flat shading, light, navy background); each card gets
a creature **of its own**: species, silhouette, pose and small scene drawn from what the card depicts. The family
shows in the palette and motifs, the rarity in the intensity of the light.

Each prompt assembles:

1. the common **opening**, which says to keep only the rendering of the reference image;
2. the **subject**: the card's creature ("Creature" line below);
3. the meaning of the card (its "What it means", in French, for context);
4. the **rarity**: sober (common), a few glints (rare), an aura and particles (epic), a radiant aura,
   particles and light rays (legendary);
5. the family's **style block** (palette, glow, motifs) and the prohibitions (text, logos, real people).

## How to use

1. In the project's Gemini conversation, attach the image of **SFTP** (`2-sftp.jpeg`, the rendering reference) and paste
   the card's prompt. For an **evolution** (26, 39, 40), also attach the image of its original card (9, 31, 18) as a second reference image.
2. Look at the image before keeping it: no text or logo, a creature clearly different from the previous ones, family and
   rarity readable at small size. Otherwise, regenerate or rework the subject (only the "Subject" sentence changes).
3. Download the image (JPEG 2400 × 1792), archive the original outside the repository
   (`originaux/<number>-<name>.jpeg`, [ADR 0015](../decisions/0015-illustrations-statiques-optimisees.md)), convert it
   to PNG in `packages/illustrations/sources/set-1/<number>.png`, describe the image in
   `packages/illustrations/set-1.json` (alternative text, brief, provenance), then run `npm run illustrations`.

Common opening, and its variant for evolutions:

```text
Generate a new image. From the reference image, keep ONLY the rendering style: line quality, soft cel shading, lighting and dark navy background treatment. Do NOT reuse its character, pose or composition: no robot standing in a doorway, no circular halo behind it, no frontal symmetric framing. Invent a creature with a clearly different body shape, size and pose, built from its own motifs, in a dynamic three-quarter view, in its own small scene. Do NOT reuse its setting either: no cable-covered floor, no punched tapes hanging on the sides, no server racks framing the picture; give the scene its own place and camera angle, drawn from the subject.
```

```text
Generate a new image. From the first reference image, keep ONLY the rendering style: line quality, soft cel shading, lighting and dark navy background treatment; do NOT reuse its character, pose or composition. The second reference image shows the base form of this card’s creature: draw its evolved form, keeping its species and recognizable silhouette, upgraded and more spectacular, in a new dynamic three-quarter pose, in its own small scene, with no circular halo behind it. Do NOT reuse the setting of either image: give the scene its own place.
```
